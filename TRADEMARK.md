# Trademark and branding policy

🇪🇸 [Versión en español](TRADEMARK.es.md)

The **source code** of Hydra Firewall is free software under the
[GPL‑3.0‑or‑later](LICENSE). You may use, study, modify and redistribute it under that license —
including making your own fork.

The **names and logos** are not part of that grant. Under the additional terms permitted by
section 7 of the GPL‑3.0, no rights are granted to use them except as described below.

## Protected names and marks

- **Hydra Firewall**™
- **Hydraroot**™
- The Hydra logo and app icons (for example `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/hydra_tree.png`,
  `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ic_header_logo.png` and the `ic_launcher*` files under
  `app/src/main/res/mipmap-*`).

They identify the official project and the builds published by its maintainer (Hydraroot / WDS657).
This policy is not a registered-trademark claim: the names are used as common-law marks, and the
maintainer may register them in the future.

## What you may do

- **Refer to the project accurately**: "based on Hydra Firewall", "a fork of Hydra Firewall", "compatible with Hydra Firewall", or link to the repository.
- **Redistribute unmodified official builds** (the APKs published in the official repository's Releases) with their name and icon unchanged, keeping the license and notices.
- **Write about, review or discuss** the project, using its name.
- **Build it for yourself or your own testing** under the original name, as long as you do not distribute that build to others.

## What you may not do

- Distribute a **modified version** (a fork) under the names *Hydra Firewall* or *Hydraroot*, or with names that could be confused with them.
- Use the **logo or app icons** in a fork, app store listing, website or product.
- Suggest that your product, service or website is **official, endorsed or sponsored** by the project when it is not.
- Use the names in a **domain name, app ID (`applicationId`), store listing or social-media handle** in a way that could mislead users.
- Use the names to sell or promote a product, or to collect donations, as if you were the official project.

## If you make a fork

1. Choose a **different name** and a **different logo and icon**.
2. Change the `applicationId` (for example with `HYDRA_APPLICATION_ID`) so your app does not replace or conflict with the official one.
3. Keep the copyright and license notices required by the GPL‑3.0, and say clearly that your project is derived from Hydra Firewall.
4. Publish your source code under the GPL‑3.0‑or‑later.

## Why this policy exists

Hydra is a firewall: people trust it with all of their network traffic. A modified build that looks
official could be used to harm users. This policy lets anyone reuse the code while helping users
tell the official app from copies.

## Reporting misuse

If you find an app, store listing or website that uses these names or logos in a misleading way, please
open a GitHub issue in this repository (without publishing personal data), or use the repository's
private security advisory form if it involves a risk to users.

## Permission

If you want to use the name or logo in a way not covered above, ask the maintainer first. Permission is
only valid if it is given in writing by the maintainer.
